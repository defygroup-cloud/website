The Rules

Honour code of the Order
  1. Declare your round in the chat before you tee off.
  2. White tees.
  3. Play with at least one other player from this group.
  4. Best 3 scores from 6 rounds.
  5. Scores must be uploaded to MyEG.
  6. Top 12 qualify for the Berkshire Final.

Best 3 of 6. Each player's total is the sum of their three highest Stableford rounds; the counting rounds are ringed in gold. Players level on points are split by fewer rounds taken.
