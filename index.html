What Defy is, in plain terms

Defy is the insight and automation platform for agencies that punch above their weight: live competitor, audience and conversation intelligence (Defy Monitor), the ability to interrogate it inside Claude (Defy MCP), and agentic workflows that take the repetitive delivery off your team (Defy Agents) — the same capability the holding networks built for millions, without the build cost or maintenance burden.

What is Defy?

Defy is an AI insight and automation platform for marketing agencies and in-house brand teams. It combines Defy Monitor (live competitor, audience and conversation intelligence), Defy MCP (interrogate that data inside Claude, no dashboard to learn) and Defy Agents (agentic workflows for repetitive delivery), so independents get the analytical and AI capability of the holding networks without the build cost.

Who is Defy for?

Independent agencies — creative, media, CRM, influencer, PR and social shops — that compete against holding-company networks, and brands with in-house teams that want direct access to the same intelligence stack without an agency margin layered on top.

How is Defy different from building our own AI stack?

Building and maintaining an analytics and AI stack takes engineers, data pipelines and constant upkeep. Defy gives you a ready-made stack you can run for every client and category, with insight that traces back to the real published content behind it — without the development cost or the maintenance burden.

Which brands and agencies use Defy?

SharkNinja's internal strategy team uses Defy Monitor to understand LATAM markets and build test-and-learn strategies. UK CRM agency Enchant uses Defy Monitor to automate CRM insight and campaign deployment for all its clients.

Who founded Defy?

Defy was founded by Ian Cassidy (CEO), who spent 20 years in creative agencies, built and scaled SHARE Creative and led a nine-figure private-equity exit to Bridgepoint, and Alek Farseev (CTO), an AI professor based in Singapore who leads all of Defy's technical development.
