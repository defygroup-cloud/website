00 — How the deck gets built

From a Defy Workspace
to a Finished, QA‑Passed Deck

Two orchestrators — /defy-brand-deck and /defy-agency-deck — run the same evidence pipeline against the Defy connector. They listen to a category through Defy studies, turn the signal into insight chains, propositions and a dated calendar, then render and QA a slide deck. They share thirteen steps and split at only three forks. One becomes a rigorous cross‑market briefing. The other a tight agency pitch.

01 — The two deliverables

Same Analysis. Two Different Decks.

Both orchestrators compute the complete analysis. What changes is the presentation — who the deck is for, and what it has to do in the room.

Cross-market briefing
/defy-brand-deck

The Rigour-and-Audience Deliverable

Audience: the brand & its market leaders Posture: rigour + audience ~32 slides
  • Leads with the audience — personas first; tensions and propositions hang off them.
  • Shows the persona → tension → angle → proposition engine as layers. Coverage is the credibility argument.
  • Carries share-of-voice and engagement, prior-year-verified trends, a brand-permission beat and an integration beat.
  • Keeps every ID — a full audit trail back to the workspace.
Agency pitch
/defy-agency-deck

The Persuasion-and-Activation Deliverable

Audience: a client being sold an idea Posture: persuasion + activation ~16–20 slides
  • Big idea on slide 1. One challenger throughline carries the whole deck.
  • Suppresses the machinery — persona names and theme IDs become sharp insight statements.
  • One clean share-of-voice visual. Not a dashboard.
  • A concrete creative or activation example per pillar the client can picture.

03 — Step by step

What Each Step Does.

Every step writes its output to disk as JSON, so paths pass cleanly between steps and a run can resume if it is interrupted. The connector tool each step calls — and the file it leaves behind — are noted on every card.

00
Scope elicitation
skill · scope-elicitation — the Step-0 gate for both orchestrators

Reads the request and asks only the questions the prompt cannot answer — never more. The default posture is full analysis on, expensive per-post outputs off, and names rather than IDs. The resulting scope.json drives every downstream step and is read again by the QA gate, so completeness is measured against what was actually agreed, not a guess.

No connector call → scope.json
01
Brand extraction
skill · brand-extraction-from-web

Pulls the client's real palette, type hints, voice, factual claims — phone, email, addresses, calls to action — and product offerings straight from their live website. The deck then looks and sounds like them, not like a template.

Web fetch — no connector call → brand.json
02
Interval splitting
orchestrator computation

Splits the analysis window into N trend windows, sized so themes have time to stabilise — each window roughly three weeks or more. This defines the time-series the rest of the run is built on.

No connector call → trend_windows.json
03
Trend studies — primary
Defy connector

Fires one Defy study per window across the brand and competitor profiles, each generating a persona report and a tensions report. All studies fire in parallel and return a study ID at once — the work runs async.

Defy:studies-create → studies_primary.json
03B
Creator-panel studies optional
Defy connector · only if an influencer workspace is supplied

Runs the same window cuts on a separate content-proxy workspace, plus one extra live 30-day study for topicality. This is a sample of content made for the target audience — never the audience itself.

Defy:studies-create → studies_influencer.json
04
Poll all studies
Defy connector

Once the async studies have had time to finish — about 90 seconds — retrieves every study in a single batch call rather than polling them one at a time.

Defy:studies-get → study results
05
Trend analysis + prior-year verification
skill · trend-analysis

Aligns themes across windows — theme IDs differ between studies — builds a time-series matrix, and classifies each tension as Rising, Falling, Sustained, Seasonal, Emerging or decayed. Before any direction is shown as a trend, a prior-year pass re-runs the same windows one year earlier to prove the move is real and not a seasonal slope. Anything that cannot be verified is flagged honestly, never shown as a confident decline. Runs twice when a creator panel exists — brand cohort and creator cohort.

Defy:studies-create / studies-get (prior-year windows) → trends_primary.json + trends_influencer.json (if panel)
06
Whitespace cohort studies
Defy connector

Three snapshot studies over the last 90 days — brand voice, competitor coverage, and optionally the creator panel — so the analysis can compare who is publishing toward what right now.

Defy:studies-create → cohort study IDs
07
Whitespace analysis
skill · whitespace-analysis

Maps themes across the cohorts, computes each cohort's share-of-voice, and assigns every theme one of six verdicts: Whitespace, Contested, Blocked, Audience-Left, Partial or Emerging. Three cohorts give full coverage; two give partial.

Analysis — no connector call → whitespace.json
08
Insight creation Fork 1
Defy connector · skill · insight-creation-brand / insight-creation-agency

For the top pillars — usually three, a mix of one whitespace, one claimable, one emerging — builds the creative DNA: a named character, a strategic angle, and a finished insight story. This is the first point the two decks diverge.

/defy-brand-deck — briefing

Keeps the full persona → tension → angle → story chain with IDs and presents it as layers — one persona mapping to many tensions. The permutation engine is the credibility argument.

/defy-agency-deck — pitch

Collapses each chain into a single pitchable insight statement. Persona names and theme IDs are suppressed from the slide and survive only in the audit backup.

Defy:persona-create_characters Defy:theme-generate_angles Defy:story-create → insights.json
09
Product + propositions
Defy connector · skill · proposition-creation

Checks that a Defy product exists for the brand — creating one per product line from the brand's offerings if not — then generates propositions from the story IDs against the focus dimensions: product features, reason-to-believe, USP, promotion. Every proposition is later shown on the slide by its real name and insight tagline retrieved from the connector, never as a bare ID, and every proposition that was generated must reach the deck.

Defy:products-list Defy:product-create Defy:propositions-create Defy:propositions-list → propositions.json
10
Moment generation
Defy connector · skill · moment-generation

Turns each persona-and-tension pair into calendar-anchored moments across moment types — life, seasonal, purchase, micro, search, emotional, vulnerable — surfacing the specific triggers when the brand can credibly enter the conversation. Supply is balanced against weeks times cadence.

Defy:moments-create → moments.json
11
Content calendar build
skill · content-calendar-build

Lays the moments onto a dated posting schedule: cadence times the derived week count, pillar weighting taken from the whitespace verdicts, cultural anchors for the locale, and a lead post per period.

Build script — no connector call → calendar.json
12
Briefs + deck render Fork 2
skill · content-brief-brand / content-brief-agency → data-driven PPTX build → PDF

Writes a publishable brief for each calendar slot, then renders the deck with a brand-agnostic, data-driven builder that reads every figure from the JSONs — identity from brand.json, never hardcoded — and converts the result to PDF. This is where the two layouts part ways.

/defy-brand-deck — briefing layout

~32 slides. Opens on the competitive landscape and audience, leads with personas, shows the engine as layers, carries share-of-voice and engagement, brand-permission and integration beats, and a full audit trail. Optionally adds a Creative-Library appendix (Step 12B).

/defy-agency-deck — pitch layout

~16–20 slides. Big idea on slide 1, one challenger throughline, one clean share-of-voice visual, an activation example per pillar. Methodology surfaces are cut.

Render uses IDs already gathered — no new connector call → deck.pptx + deck.pdf
12B
Creative expressions optional · brief only
Defy connector · skill · creative-expressions

If the creative-expressions skill is installed, renders each concept into an image and assembles a grouped Creative-Library appendix — one grid per pillar across TOFU, MOFU and BOFU. If the skill is absent the deck ships text-only: a fully supported fallback, not a degraded deck.

Defy:image-generate → expr/ PNGs + appendix
13
QA gate — mandatory Fork 3
skill · deck-qa-brand / deck-qa-agency

The deck is not finished when the PDF renders. It is finished when QA passes. The variant QA skill verifies every figure against its source data, chart integrity, on-brand wordmark and category colour-coding, claim honesty, and completeness against the agreed scope — returning a PASS or FAIL with a defect list.

deck-qa-brand — briefing checks

Personas lead, engine shown as layers, share-of-voice not raw counts, prior-year-verified trends, honest whitespace, and a full audit trail with IDs kept.

deck-qa-agency — pitch checks

Big idea on slide 1, no persona names or theme IDs on body slides, one clean share-of-voice visual, an activation example per pillar, and ~16–20 slide length.

Verification — no connector call FAIL → fix the source JSON or the builder, rebuild from Step 12, re-run the full checklist. No iteration cap.

06 — Honest by design

The Connector Measures Content, Not People.

Content supply — not audience demand.

Defy classifies what posts are about and who they were made for. It does not observe the audience directly. So a deck speaks in terms of content supply — "content made for Foodies", "what creators publish toward this audience" — rather than audience demand, and a whitespace gap is a content-supply gap, not unmet demand. The one audience-side signal is views and engagement volume. Both QA gates fail any slide that overclaims. That discipline is exactly what makes the output safe to put in front of a client.

07 — What comes out

A Deck You Can Defend, Line by Line

Every figure traces to an analysis JSON. Every claim traces to a Defy object. The deck survives the room. Same engine, two finishes — pick the one the audience needs.

The QA gate is the contract. Nothing is delivered, presented, or described as finished until deck-qa-brand or deck-qa-agency returns PASS. A FAIL sends the run back to fix the source data or the builder and rebuild — never to hand-patch a rendered slide — and the full checklist re-runs until it passes.
